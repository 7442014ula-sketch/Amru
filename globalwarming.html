GLOBAL WARMING

a gradual increase in the overall temperature of the earth's atmosphere generally attributed to the greenhouse effect caused by increased levels of carbon dioxide, CFCs, and other pollutants:

SOME INTESTING FACTS

  1. Global Warming Is Primarily Caused by Human Activities
  2. Carbon Dioxide Levels Are the Highest in Millions of Years
  3. The Planet’s Average Temperature Has Already Increased
  4. The Oceans Are Absorbing Most of the Heat

CAUSES OF GLOBAL WARMING

  1. Burning Fossil Fuels
  2. Deforestation
  3. Agriculture and Livestock
  4. Industrial Processes
  5. greenhouse gas emissions
  6. Transportation

PREVENTION

Transition to Clean Energy
Switching from fossil fuels to renewable energy sources like solar, wind, and nuclear power is the most impactful way to reduce global carbon emissions. Replacing coal, oil, and natural gas power plants with clean energy can significantly cut greenhouse gases, and modernizing power grids and adding battery storage ensures reliable energy supply even when renewable generation fluctuates scienceinsights.org scienceinsights.org +1 . Efficiency and Conservation
Improving energy efficiency in homes and buildings reduces emissions. This includes insulating homes, sealing drafts, upgrading to energy-efficient appliances, and using LED lighting. Even small actions like unplugging devices when not in use or washing clothes in cold water can lower energy consumption NOAA Climate.gov NOAA Climate.gov +2 . Sustainable Transportation
Transportation is a major source of emissions. Driving electric vehicles, using public transit, carpooling, biking, or walking can reduce your carbon footprint. For longer distances, trains and buses are more sustainable than individual car travel
